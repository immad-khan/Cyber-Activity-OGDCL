Activity 3: Social Engineering Defense

Recognize Manipulation. Verify Identity. Protect OGDCL.

Social Engineering Defense Landscape

Video: 50 marks Quiz: 100 marks

Key Learning Points

  • Psychological Exploitation: Attackers weaponize fear, urgency, trust, and curiosity rather than software flaws. Stay vigilant.
  • Pretexting Tactics: Deceptive backstories (e.g., impersonating IT support, external auditors, or executive staff) are used to extract sensitive data.
  • Vishing & Phone Scams: Never divulge passwords, MFA codes, or OTPs over phone calls. Legitimate OGDCL IT will never ask for credentials.
  • Baiting Hazards: Unlabeled USB drives or media dropped in lobbies and parking lots contain malware. Immediately surrender found media to IT Security.
  • Tailgating Prevention: Never hold secure facility doors for unfamiliar or unbadged individuals. Every employee must badge in individually.
  • Authority Bias: High-pressure requests invoking corporate executive authority must always be verified via official corporate channels.
  • Prompt Escalation: Reporting suspicious interactions to the Information Security Division prevents enterprise-wide compromise.
