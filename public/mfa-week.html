🔐 Key Learning Points

  • Strong Passwords: Use at least 12 characters with upper/lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Avoid personal info.
  • Two-Factor Authentication: 2FA adds an extra layer of security even if your password is stolen. Blocks 99% of hacks.
  • Password Managers: Use Bitwarden or 1Password to store passwords securely. Generate strong, unique passwords automatically.
  • Never Reuse: Create unique passwords for each account. One breach shouldn't compromise all your accounts.
  • Browser Safety: Don't save passwords in browsers. Password managers offer stronger encryption.
  • 80% Statistic: Over 80% of breaches involve weak or stolen passwords. Your password is your first defense.
  • Report Suspicious Activity: Always report suspicious login attempts or unexpected MFA prompts immediately.
  • Regular Updates: Change compromised passwords immediately and review old, unused accounts regularly.
