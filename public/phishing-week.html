Activity 1: The Executive Target

When You Lead, You're on the Front Line.

Executive Threat Landscape

Video: 50 marks Quiz: 100 marks

Key Learning Points

  • Business Email Compromise: Attackers impersonate executives to request wire transfers. Always verify via phone.
  • AI Deepfakes: Voice and video can be faked. Establish verification codes for sensitive requests.
  • Credential Theft: Use hardware MFA keys and unique passwords. Never reuse credentials.
  • Executive Risk: 71% of targeted attacks focus on C-level. You are 4.6x more likely to be targeted.
  • Verification Protocol: When in doubt, call back using a known number before acting on requests.
  • Red Flags: Unexpected MFA prompts, urgent financial requests, poor video quality, or unusual login alerts.
  • Best Practice: Separate work and personal accounts. Use VPN on public networks.
  • Cost of Breach: Average executive account compromise costs $3.9M. Your security protects the organization.
