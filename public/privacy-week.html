Video Key Topics & Privacy Rules

  • Data TrackingDigital Footprint: Websites, cookies, and mobile apps constantly track and aggregate personal habits and location history.
  • PII ProtectionPersonally Identifiable Information: Keep CNIC, financial, medical, and corporate confidential data encrypted and confidential.
  • Public Wi-FiNetwork Encryption: Public Wi-Fi networks in airports and cafes expose unencrypted traffic; always use an authenticated VPN.
  • Data DefenseMFA & Clean Desk: Enable multi-factor authentication, use strong unique passwords, and lock workstations (Win + L) whenever unattended.
  • Least PrivilegeNeed-to-Know Access: Limit app permissions and grant users only the minimum access necessary for their specific job duties.
